Who we are

An Indian Private Limited Company built for the long term.

Building financial confidence through transparency, integrity and long-term value creation.

SAINI ENGG. & FINANCE PRIVATE LIMITED was incorporated on 29 August 1996 and is registered with the Registrar of Companies, Kolkata. The company has operated for nearly three decades in business consulting, financial advisory and corporate services, guided by a small, stable board that has been in place since inception.

We are not currently a registered NBFC. Where this page refers to lending or investment-style products, they are marked clearly as Upcoming Services on our services page and will only be activated once the relevant regulatory approvals are in place.
