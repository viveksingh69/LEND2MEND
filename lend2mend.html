Lend2Mend is a technology-driven Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) committed to providing accessible, transparent, and customer-centric financial solutions to individuals, professionals, and businesses. Our diverse portfolio of lending products is designed to meet evolving financial needs while promoting responsible borrowing and sustainable growth.

Personal Loans

  • Unsecured loans for medical emergencies, education, home renovation, travel, weddings, and other personal requirements.
  • Quick approvals, minimal documentation, and flexible repayment options.

Business Loans

  • Working capital and growth financing for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).
  • Funding for inventory, machinery, equipment, business expansion, and operational expenses.

MSME Loans

  • Customized financing solutions to support entrepreneurs and small businesses.
  • Competitive pricing and flexible loan structures based on business cash flows.

Loan Against Property (LAP)

  • Secured loans against residential or commercial property.
  • Suitable for business expansion, education, debt consolidation, or other financial needs.

Gold Loans

  • Fast and secure loans against gold ornaments.
  • Simple documentation with quick loan disbursement.

Supply Chain & Vendor Finance

  • Working capital solutions for manufacturers, distributors, dealers, and suppliers.
  • Improved cash flow through timely financing.

Digital Lending

  • Fully digital loan application and approval process.
  • Paperless documentation, e-KYC, online loan tracking, and faster disbursement.

Financial Advisory Services

  • Personalized financial guidance to help customers select suitable credit solutions and manage borrowing effectively.
